Assess this idea: $ARGUMENTS

Identify the intended outcome, audience, constraints, and domain from the
context. Ask only questions whose answers could materially change the
assessment. Be direct and proportionate to the evidence; do not sugarcoat, but
do not present speculation as fact.

For a business idea, research the relevant market, alternatives or competitors,
customer evidence, feasibility, costs, risks, and blind spots when current
external information would help. For other kinds of ideas, use criteria suited
to that domain rather than forcing a market analysis.

Separate sourced facts, assumptions, estimates, and unknowns. Cite sources for
current claims. Avoid an arbitrary overall score; if a score is requested,
define its criteria and limits. Recommend practical improvements when supported
by evidence, then propose a small real-world validation experiment with an
observable success or stop signal.
